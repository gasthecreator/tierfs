# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/): be respectful, assume good faith, and keep
the focus on the work.

**Expected:** welcoming and inclusive language; respect for differing viewpoints and experience levels; graciously accepting constructive
criticism; focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, insults, trolling, publishing others' private information, or anything that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement:** the maintainer may edit, hide, or remove comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily
or permanently ban contributors. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@gasthecreator](https://github.com/gasthecreator))
or through a private security advisory on this repository. Reports are handled confidentially.

This applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
